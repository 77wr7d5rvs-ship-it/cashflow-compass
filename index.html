TODAY’S BIG IDEA

Profit is not cash.

A company can make a profit on paper but still run short of cash. The P&L explains performance; cash flow explains movement of money.

Revenue − costs = profit
Cash flow = cash in − cash out

THE FOUNDATIONS

Build the picture first.

P&L

Profit & Loss statement

A report card for a period: what a business earned, spent, and the profit left over.

CASH FLOW

Cash flow statement

A bank-account story: cash generated and used in operating, investing, and financing activities.

THE LINK

Why they differ

Accounting records when value is earned or used. Cash flow records when money moves.
